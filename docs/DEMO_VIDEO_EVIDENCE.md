# Demo video evidence

`verify-screenshots/current/demo-submission.mp4` is a machine-assembled visual demo artifact built from current product screenshots and the 180-second shot budget in `docs/DEMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`.

It is useful for reviewing the shipped UI states and provenance labels, but it is not claimed as a human-narrated, human-replayed competition submission until a person reviews the final artifact and records any required narration.

Machine verification: H.264, 1280×720, 30 fps, exactly 5,400 frames and 180.000 seconds (`ffprobe`). The artifact has no audio track; narration and final human replay remain open acceptance items.
